# Business Insights

## 1.key findings
### Finding 1:
The total revenue for the three-month period was 457,600,000 from 147 units sold across 15 transactions. February generated the highest monthly revenue at 178,200,000, with month-over-month revenue growth of 15.7%. In March, the quantity sold increased to 59 units, but revenue declined by 29.6% compared with February.
### Finding 2:
Laptops generated the highest revenue contributing 360,000,000, or approximately 78.8% of total revenue.
### Finding 3:
The top three products by revenue accounted for approximately 97.6% of total revenue, while the remaining product contributed only about 2.4%.

## 2.Business Recommendations
### Recommendation 1:
Review the sales performance of products outside the top three to identify opportunities for growth. Investigate whether differences in selling prices, sales volumes, or customer demand explain their relatively low contribution to total revenue.

### Recommendation 2:
Because laptops account for a large share of total revenue, the business may be exposed to product concentration risk. Consider evaluating opportunities to diversify revenue sources while assessing customer demand, profit margins, and supply constraints before making inventory decisions.

## 3.Limitations
This analysis covers only three months of data and does not include profit margins, costs, inventory levels, or customer demand. Therefore, revenue figures alone are insufficient to determine product profitability or make definitive inventory decisions. Further analysis by unit price, sales volume, and profit margin would provide more actionable insights.
